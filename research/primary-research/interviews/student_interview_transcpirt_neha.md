## Respondent: Daily eater — 1 (unlabeled in source)

**How many mornings a week do you eat breakfast at the mess, and how many do you skip?**
I don't skip breakfast generally. Mostly, I skip on Sundays. Some days I have
breakfast from outside. Other than that, I have breakfast almost every day, except
for maybe one or two days.

**On a morning you skip, what's the usual reason?**
Maybe if I've stayed up late because of assignments or classes, or if I sleep around
3:00 or 4:00 a.m. or later, I don't tend to wake up. I prefer sleep over breakfast.
Other than that, in all cases I generally have breakfast.

**On a morning you go, what makes you decide to go right then?**
There's no other reason. I generally prefer to have my breakfast to start my day.

**Do you check the app before deciding to go or skip?**
I didn't specifically mention checking the app before deciding whether to go or
skip. There is an option in the app to check the mess crowd, but I rarely check it.

**Does anyone else, like a roommate or friend, factor into whether you go?**
No. I generally go alone for breakfast, so it doesn't matter.

**If that person was away for a week, do you think your attendance would change?**
No, it wouldn't really matter.

**What's the queue/counter usually like when you get there?**
If I have an 8:30 class, I usually go around 7:30 or 8:00. The counter is very
peaceful then, and I can get whatever I want. But most days I go around 9:00 to
9:30, and it is usually very crowded. Especially after 9:15, it's very crowded, and
it's not guaranteed that I'll get all the things I want.

**Has an item ever run out by the time you got there — which one, roughly when?**
Yes. Most of the time when I'm at Kadamba around 9:20, the main items are already
finished. Things like idli, puri, and bhatura often run out. The fruits also get
taken away, and they don't always provide new fruits. Many times, items have run
out, and the staff has promised to bring them back before the mess closes at 9:30,
but they don't tend to return the items that have run out.

**Do you register day-by-day or all at once for the week?**
I register for a month, not day by day. I usually register for Kadamba or Bugul for
the whole month.

**Have you ever cancelled a registration?**
Yes, I cancel registrations, probably around five times.

**Have you used "Skip Meal"? What do you think it actually does?**
I never use Skip Meal because it doesn't return the money. It just lets them know
that the meal can be skipped, whereas they are already preparing food for only some
percentage of the registered students.

**Have you ever bought or sold a registration on Mess Cell WhatsApp?**
Yes, I have both bought and sold registrations.

**Have you ever eaten as a walk-in without being registered?**
Yes, I've walked in and eaten without being registered for snacks, but not for
breakfast.

**Do you ever register somewhere just in case, planning to resell it if you don't go?**
No, I don't generally do that. I register for a month, and if I don't feel like
eating, I may sell the registration. Sometimes I don't sell it either.

**Has how often you eat breakfast changed this semester — since when?**
Yes. This semester I've been more regular with eating breakfast.

**Do you have a sense of when the mess is busiest?**
Yes. There is an option in the app to check the mess crowd, but I rarely check it. I
don't really care if the mess is busy.

**Do you usually eat with others — does that decide whether you go?**
No. I prefer eating alone. If somebody is there, I'll eat with them, but that
doesn't decide whether I go or not.

**Have you or anyone you know tried to get a mess rule/timing changed? What happened?**
No. Sometimes when food runs out or some technical errors happen, the mess makes
changes, but those are usually just immediate changes related to those particular
issues.

**Do you think students have real input in mess decisions?**
Maybe students have some input in things like menu-making, but other than that, for
institutional or organizational-level changes, I don't think students really have
much input.

**Would breakfast starting an hour later change anything for you?**
Not starting an hour later, but ending an hour later would help me.

**How much do you think you've spent this semester on breakfasts you didn't eat?**
Not much, especially this semester. It might be less than ₹500 because I'm a
regular breakfast eater.

**Anything else about breakfast/mess worth mentioning?**
They should prepare food for more people, and they should increase the breakfast
timing. It's an absolute waste of money for almost everyone because most people
don't actually eat breakfast, and the current system doesn't seem to account
properly for the people who do come.

## Respondent: Daily eater — 2 / Daily eater — 3

*(Both labels appear consecutively in the source document, immediately followed by
one shared transcript below — preserved exactly as it appears in the source.)*

**How many mornings a week do you eat breakfast at the mess, and how many do you skip?**
I eat breakfast at the mess on most days. I do sometimes miss breakfast, especially
when I have an early 8:30 class or when I've stayed up very late because of
assignments, deadlines, or other commitments. If I can't wake up by around 9:00, I
might end up skipping it.

**On a morning you skip, what's the usual reason?**
Usually, it's because I've slept very late, sometimes around 3:00 or 4:00 a.m., and
then I'm not able to wake up in time. If I have an 8:30 class and I wake up around
8:00 or 8:30, I prioritize going to class because of the attendance policy rather
than going for breakfast and coming back.

**On a morning you go, what makes you decide to go right then?**
I usually decide to go once I'm properly awake and alert, around 8:30 or so.
Sometimes I spend 10–30 minutes scrolling through my phone, reading messages, or
looking at the news. Then I brush my teeth and plan to leave the hostel by around
9:00 so that I can reach Kadamba within 5–10 minutes. I generally make sure I get my
breakfast before moving on to my other commitments.

**Do you check the app before deciding to go or skip?**
I'm registered for breakfast, but I didn't specifically mention checking the app
before deciding whether to go. My decision is mostly based on my schedule and
whether I'm able to wake up in time.

**Does anyone else, like a roommate or friend, factor into whether you go?**
Sometimes. I try to wake up my friends and encourage them to come with me. But if
they don't wake up, I prioritize my own breakfast and leave. I'll tell them to get
ready and catch up later.

**If that person was away for a week, do you think your attendance would change?**
Probably not. I might prefer eating with my friends, but even if they don't come,
I'll still prioritize having my breakfast.

**What's the queue/counter usually like when you get there?**
I usually plan to reach Kadamba around 9:00 to 9:30. During that time, there can be
a lot of crowding. The demand is high and many people start queuing around the same
time, so the queue can become quite long.

**Has an item ever run out by the time you got there — which one, roughly when?**
Yes, I've experienced situations where the food supply becomes very low around 9:00
to 9:30. Since there are many people queuing and the demand is high, the available
food can become insufficient. Sometimes the mess may not be able to arrange more
food quickly enough, which creates a bad experience.

**Do you register day-by-day or all at once for the week?**
I'm generally registered for breakfast. The interview doesn't specify whether I
register day-by-day or all at once.

**Have you ever cancelled a registration?**
I haven't specifically mentioned cancelling registrations. Sometimes I
unintentionally miss breakfast because I'm unable to wake up on time, rather than
deliberately planning to skip it.

**Have you used "Skip Meal"? What do you think it actually does?**
I haven't specifically mentioned using Skip Meal.

**Have you ever bought or sold a registration on Mess Cell WhatsApp?**
I haven't mentioned buying or selling registrations on WhatsApp.

**Have you ever eaten as a walk-in without being registered?**
I haven't mentioned eating as a walk-in.

**Do you ever register somewhere just in case, planning to resell it if you don't go?**
I haven't mentioned doing this.

**Has how often you eat breakfast changed this semester — since when?**
I generally eat breakfast on most days, although I occasionally miss it because of
late nights, assignments, deadlines, or early classes.

**Do you have a sense of when the mess is busiest?**
Yes. Around 9:00 to 9:30, there seems to be a lot of crowding because many people
start queuing around the same time. The demand is high during this period.

**Do you usually eat with others — does that decide whether you go?**
I try to eat with my friends and usually try to wake them up so they can come with
me. But it doesn't determine whether I go. If they don't wake up, I'll still go and
have breakfast by myself.

**Have you or anyone you know tried to get a mess rule/timing changed? What happened?**
I haven't personally mentioned trying to change the mess rules or timings. However,
I think there should be some organizational-level changes to address the breakfast
supply problem. If the statistics show that only around 30% of registered students
actually come for breakfast, but the mess still isn't able to arrange enough food
for those who do come, management should look into it.

**Do you think students have real input in mess decisions?**
I haven't directly mentioned whether students have real input, but I feel that
management needs to be more involved in addressing the issues students experience,
especially with food availability and organization.

**Would breakfast starting an hour later change anything for you?**
I haven't specifically said that starting breakfast an hour later would change my
attendance. My main issue is being able to fit breakfast around early classes and my
morning schedule. Better timing or a more flexible breakfast window could
potentially help on mornings when I wake up late.

**How much do you think you've spent this semester on breakfasts you didn't eat?**
I haven't mentioned a specific amount.

**Anything else about breakfast/mess worth mentioning?**
The quality of the breakfast at the mess is generally decent, and that's one reason
I continue to prefer eating at Kadamba. However, the biggest issue is the mismatch
between demand and food availability around 9:00 to 9:30. There can be a large
crowd, while the food supply is already running low. Even though only around 30% of
registered students may actually show up, the mess sometimes still struggles to
arrange enough food for the students who do come. I think this needs to be
addressed at an organizational or management level so that the food supply better
matches actual demand and students don't have a bad experience after making the
effort to come for breakfast.

I don't really check the app for that.

**Have you ever cancelled a registration?**

No, I've never used Skip Meal.

**Have you ever bought or sold a registration on Mess Cell WhatsApp?**
Yes, but rarely.

**Have you ever eaten as a walk-in without being registered?**
