**Source:** first-hand account from a team member (sricharanperi) of the September 2026 visits to Kadamba and Yuktahaar, given on 2026-09-27. The team member was present for the operator conversations recorded in the audio files. It is kept verbatim per D-4/D-41; the only edits are this header and one bracketed note on a name the speaker corrected mid-account. The structured reading, and its reconciliation with the photographed registers and the recordings, is in `../2026-09-27_team-field-account-evidence-brief.md`.
