# Quick Interview Questions Reference (neha, 2026-09-06)

Condensed, for sending over text/WhatsApp where a
short written reply is more realistic than a live 20-30 min conversation. Drops the "walk
me through minute-by-minute" narrative framing in favor of direct questions; use the full
guide for live interviews.

---

## Students (Guide 1)

1. How many mornings a week do you eat breakfast at the mess?
2. On a morning you skip, what usually happens instead?
3. On a morning you go, what makes you decide to go right then?
4. Do you check the app before deciding?
5. Does anyone else affect whether you go (roommate/friend)?
6. If that person was away a week, would your attendance change?
7. What's the queue usually like when you get there?
8. Has an item ever run out — which one, roughly when?
9. Do you register day-by-day or all at once for the week?
10. Have you ever cancelled a registration?
11. Have you used "Skip Meal"? What do you think it actually does?
12. Have you bought or sold a registration on the Mess Cell WhatsApp group?
13. Have you eaten as a walk-in without being registered?
14. Do you ever register just in case, planning to resell it if you don't go?
15. Has your attendance changed this semester — since when?
16. Do you know when the mess is busiest?
17. Do you eat with others — does that decide whether you go?
18. Have you or anyone you know tried to get a mess rule changed? What happened?
19. Do you think students have real input in mess decisions?
20. Would breakfast starting an hour later change anything for you?
21. What actually decides which mess you register at?
22. How much do you think you've spent this semester on breakfasts you didn't eat?
23. Anything else worth mentioning?
