# Conclusion and Future Work {#ch-conclusion}

## Conclusion {#sec-conclusion}

The project began from the claim that students skip breakfast because early classes collide with late nights, and it ended with a different account. Breakfast registration at IIIT Hyderabad is a default that no one chose to keep, the charge falls on the registration whether or not the meal is eaten, and each kitchen guesses how many will come while the records that could correct the guess are never delivered to those who set the rules. About 63 per cent of Kadamba's breakfast registrations in April 2026 went unused, and about 72 per cent at Yuktāhār in September, while lunch under the same rules worked. The paradox describes a stable system. Every actor behaves reasonably, and each consequence is absorbed before it reaches the people who could change the rules.

The recommended response has three parts that run together: build the missing routes through a weekly ledger and joint readings, change the default with a Sunday skip on a fixed date, and tune the kitchen alongside, labelled as the quick fix. A pilot on Kadamba's vegetarian Sunday line is designed, with success and stop criteria, and the model projects a large change in the kitchen and a small one for students until the rule moves. All of this remains projected, simulated or planned, and none of it has yet been tested on the campus.

## Future Work {#sec-future}

Several steps would move the work forward. The first is to obtain what the records would show: breakfast scans for months other than April, lunch and dinner exports, scan records for Yuktāhār, Bakul and Palash, and an item-level record for Bakul. The second is to settle the payment basis for each operator, since it gates the held levers. The third is to run the co-design tests with a supervisor and a ledger keeper, which are planned and have not been done. The fourth is the pilot itself, with its stop conditions. A fifth is to test the Sunday skip against student uptake, which the model assumes and has not observed. The last is to extend the analysis to lunch and dinner, which were kept outside the boundary as a control.
