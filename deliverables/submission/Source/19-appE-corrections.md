# @Corrections to the First Submissions {#app-corrections}

Every correction made after the first submissions is recorded once, with the first claim and what replaced it. In each case the replacement was made in every deliverable in the same pass. The corrections are grouped by phase; their stories are told in [[sec:beliefs]].
