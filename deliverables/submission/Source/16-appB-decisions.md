# @Decision Register {#app-decisions}

The register lists the decisions taken during the project in the order they were made. It covers decisions about scope, evidence, method, analysis, design and the conduct of the documentation. Decisions about software and file handling are left out, since they do not affect what the project found. Where a later decision changed an earlier one, both rows are kept. The reason column gives the reason recorded at the time, and the last column gives the section where the decision applies. The sections of the main text also mark each decision where it was taken.
