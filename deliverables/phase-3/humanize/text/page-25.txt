Aflat projection under-reads because only 13.5 to 22.5 percent of diners have actually arrived
by 8:00, not the quarter it assumes. The weekday profile removes most of the bias but not the
noise, since the early count is still small, and reading the count at 9:00 instead halves the error
again. Hence the 9:00 trigger, with the card rebuilt monthly.
Scenario 3. One Sunday on the veg line. Across three backtest Sundays, the baseline
cooked about 563 for about 190 eaten, a surplus of about 373, or 2.5 to 3.4 times what was
eaten across ratios of 60 to 80 percent. The re ned design would cook about 233, leaving
about 45 surplus and about 2 short. Sunday veg is the week’s largest single surplus block, and
the natural pilot slice.
