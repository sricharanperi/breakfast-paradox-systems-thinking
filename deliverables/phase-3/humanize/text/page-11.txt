1.3 Develop Design Principles
The objective is a breakfast system where registration matches each student’s actual intent,
the kitchen cooks to expected eaters, and the cost of any gap reaches the people who set the
rules. It doesn’t mean eliminating registration, resale or all waste. Registration gives the
planning count, resale recovers money, and some surplus is simply the price of never running
out. The aim is to make over-booking and over-cooking exceptions someone notices, not the
norm. The guiding idea follows straight from the archetype reading: change the architecture,
don’t compete at the kitchen layer.
