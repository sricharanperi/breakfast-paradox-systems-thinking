Check reliance on resale

Across the whole set, only LP5 and LP6 actually touch an absorber that protects someone,
and both are written specifically to keep it intact. LP5 budgets outside diners and half of
Kadamba’s staff by name and keeps reuse, the walk-in buffer and the top-up reserve; LP6
keeps them too and makes late and back-door service explicit with a late batch. LP2 and LP10
shrink resale by removing the need for it, not the market itself. Repeated reheating and the bin
get recorded by LP1, reduced directly by LP3 through LP6, and reduced upstream by LP2,
LP10, LP11 and LP12. No lever removes an absorber just to create a signal, but that’s not the
same as saying no lever costs anyone anything.
