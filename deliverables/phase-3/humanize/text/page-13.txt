1.5 Deliverable 1: Leverage-Point / System Intervention Map
The map draws the breakfast system as one chain, from the automatic default through the
day-before plan, the first batch, service and the surplus, down to the bin, with records and
money running above it and governance at the top. Markers are coloured by place. LP5 and
LP6 each appear once per part, LP3 is marked by its deeper part, and a dashed ring marks a
held lever. Dashed red lines are routes that don’t exist today and that a lever would need to
build; the dashed green line is the learning link that runs at Yuktāhār and is absent at
Kadamba. Grey tags carry the intervention points, with IP13 and IP14 sitting at the governance
bodies the dashed routes would reach.
