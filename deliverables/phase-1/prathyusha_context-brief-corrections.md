## Correction 1 — the four messes are organized by cuisine, not just by veg/non-veg capability

`01-system-context-brief.md` §3 currently describes Kadamba, Palash, Bakul, and Yuktāhār
without a cuisine dimension. Corrected structure, per direct user confirmation
(2026-09-05):

- **Kadamba** — South Indian, veg + non-veg
- **Palash** — North Indian, veg-only (cannot serve non-veg — structural)
- **Bakul** — North Indian, veg + non-veg (veg cooked at Palash, shipped over; non-veg
  cooked on-site)
- **Yuktāhār** — Jain-oriented, with a separate Jain-food registration variant

**Why this matters more than a naming fix:** laid against the real breakfast uptake
numbers already in §3 (Kadamba Veg 551/700 = 79%, Kadamba Non-Veg 308/600 = 51%, Bakul
Veg 108/350 = 31%, Bakul Non-Veg 37/350 = 11%, Palāsh 146/400 = 36.5%), every North
Indian line clusters far below the one South Indian line. The existing "Bakul is newer/
a converted warehouse" explanation (§3, "why Bakul's numbers are lower") only ever
explained *one* mess. **Cuisine preference now explains the pattern across three
messes sharing a single variable** — a stronger, competing hypothesis, not a
replacement (both stay open pending direct confirmation from a student or vendor;
they aren't mutually exclusive).

## Correction 2 — governance is two actors, not one

`01-system-context-brief.md` §3 currently states: *"the Mess Committee decides
mess-specific matters... the Warden handles vendor management."* Public-source research
(Ping, IIIT-H's student publication — two articles, Dec 2024 and Jan 2025) plus direct
user confirmation reveal a split that was being collapsed into one actor:

- **Mess Committee** — a policy body, **chaired by a faculty member** (Prof. Girish, as
  of Jan 2025 — not independently re-verified for 2026), sets menu/policy with student
  input
- **Mess Office** — a separate, operational/administrative arm: places food orders
  (confirmed **4-day lead time**, up from a 2-day figure in the Dec 2024 source),
  runs the portal relationship, and sent the Dec 2024 policy-change email. Its stated
  motive for that change was explicitly reducing **staff workload**, not improving
  student experience.

Relationship between Mess Office and the Warden is still ❓ unconfirmed — may overlap.

## Correction 3 — "no escalation mechanism" was too strong; revise to "inputs exist, outputs don't"

An earlier note (added and then walked back within this same session) claimed no
negotiation/escalation mechanism could be confirmed at all. That overstated it. Real
input channels do exist:

- An **in-app per-meal rating/feedback system** (confirmed via portal screenshots)
- A **public email channel** students use to raise mess issues directly (user-confirmed)
- **Ping**, the student publication, has published at least twice on mess problems
  (Dec 2024, Jan 2025) — a real, if indirect, public-pressure channel

**What's still unconfirmed, and is now the sharpest open question in the whole
project:** whether *any* of these three channels has ever produced a concrete policy
change. No source gathered so far — real respondent, synthetic pilot, or public
research — could name a specific example either way. This is what actually explains
why the Academic Office/class-timing lever has never been pursued: it's not that
there's no way to raise an issue, it's that raising one has no confirmed downstream
effect.

## New facts, not corrections — additions the context brief doesn't have at all

- **A real, public, unofficial tool exists**: `NJP6969/IIITH-mess-MCP` on GitHub, MIT-
  licensed, lets students manage registration/cancellation/billing/feedback via an AI
  agent, "built for students of IIIT Hyderabad." You don't know of anyone using one —
  kept at low confidence, but its mere existence changes what "a student registered and
  didn't show up" could mechanically mean (a manual choice vs. an automated default).
- **Kadamba underwent a renovation** (user-confirmed) — timing and current status of any
  temporary rule changes from that period are unconfirmed.
- **Food procurement is per-mess**, not centralized (user-confirmed) — each vendor
  handles its own purchasing and cooking, not just cooking.
- **The current live portal is `dining.iiit.ac.in`** (user-confirmed) — consistent with
  what's already in the primary research screenshots; "IMS," named in the Dec 2024
  source, may be an internal/backend name for the same system rather than a different
  one — unconfirmed.
