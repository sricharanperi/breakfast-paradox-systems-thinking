## Phase 3 implications

1. **LP-E (first-batch calibration) must plan for 30 to 50 outside diners plus about half of Kadamba's staff, not 20 to 30.** The absorber is larger and partly a planned cook, so the D-45/D-49 staff-meal allowance must be explicit and sized to Brief #2's figures.
2. **The waste tension (70% vs 37% vs 5 to 10%) must be settled before any waste-reduction claim is sized.** If the surplus is absorbed by reheating rather than binned, the benefit of calibration is quality (less held food) more than tonnage, which changes the success metric (P6) and the pitch to CDS.
3. **Surplus → hold and reheat → degraded quality is a candidate link from over-preparation to the student experience** (open chain, D-40). A calibrated first batch plus a staged late batch (LP-I) could improve quality without changing registration rules; this gives the operator a reason to adopt LP-E that is not about cost.
4. **Yuktāhār's first-half-hour projection is structurally biased low** under a late-crest arrival curve (about 15% of Kadamba scans in the first half hour, 26.5% in the last quarter hour). LP-F/LP-I should propose a crest-aware projection (weekday arrival profile from the register or CDS scans) rather than a linear one.
5. **Blame-free measurement is a precondition** for any intervention that adds measurement (LP-A, LP-E, LP-K). Yuktāhār's staff resisted until it was explicitly blame-free and discussed daily.
6. **Low-digital, paper-first design.** Yuktāhār dropped Zoho on cost and staff digital capacity; interventions must work on paper books and the daily meeting, with office staff digitising later.
7. **LP-J (lateral transfer) now has concrete content:** the Yuktāhār book system (Wastage Book with run-out time, two-book reconciliation, weekday register, daily meeting) is a template Kadamba lacks, while Kadamba already has per-dish gram standards to build on.
8. **Operator culture and incentive as a lever:** Kadamba's "paid for the registration" vs Yuktāhār's explicit profit-through-less-waste goal under the same billing system supports treating vendor goal-setting as a leverage point, still subject to D-54 (payment basis unknown).
9. **Rule owner for LP-A/LP-L:** the Kadamba chain now names CDS (head Raju, conflicting with CDS Chair Giri) above Prism. Phase 3 must not name an owner until the Raju/Giri question is answered.
10. **Biryani 100% shows the ratio is demand-expectation-driven, not fixed.** An intervention that improves the operator's expectation (records by weekday and item) moves the ratio without a rule change.
11. **Back-door late arrivals are an informal grace period nobody can refuse.** LP-I's "formal grace period" would formalise an existing behaviour; design it with the late batch in mind.
12. **Kadamba "cooks on registrations" means a Skip Meal or cancellation-based lever only helps if it changes the count the operator sees**; confirm before relying on Skip Meal redesign.
