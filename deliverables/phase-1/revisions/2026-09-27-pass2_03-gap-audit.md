## Headline

Pass 1 correctly made "the operator" a data-rich, authority-poor actor. Brief #2 shows that this "operator" is really **two opposite power structures under the same billing rule**, and the deliverable still speaks of them as one:

1. **Kadamba** sits at the bottom of a six-level formal chain, **CFS → CDS (head: Raju; conflicts with the earlier "CDS Chair Giri", D-24) → Prism (tender-bid, outsourced vendor running all operations) → operations manager → manager → supervisor → service staff**. The **supervisor** is the real-time quantity decider ("always in constant check... telling them how much to cook"). Its stated stance is to cook **on registrations** because "it is a responsibility and they are getting paid for it", and it keeps per-dish gram standards and a "5-10% daily" waste figure, not a book system.
2. **Yuktāhār (ABC Hospitality Services)** is **flat**: two project managers, no hierarchy, everyone does every job, peers cross-check (Ajita keeps accounts and the Wastage Register; Bhavani cross-verifies the grocery register). It runs a ten-book record system with a two-book reconciliation rule and has an **explicit profit and cost-reduction goal** that makes waste reduction pay.

Several pass-1 sentences generalise Yuktāhār's stance ("distrusts registration, corrects it privately", "the only per-item, per-weekday record") to all operators, which Brief #2 now contradicts for Kadamba. The deliverable also still carries the Brief #1 staff-meal figures that Brief #2 corrects, "no operator cooks the full registration count" (contradicted by Kadamba biryani at 100%), and a "Kadamba records waste by weight" claim that rested on the withdrawn kg figures. Brief #2 also adds two power findings the document lacks entirely: **institutional oversight is real but covers only safety and quality** (weekly QC, per-meal facility tasting with two Prism staff, daily TDS, the 72-hour sample), never quantity or waste; and **students cannot nullify a registration** beyond five cancellations a month made four days ahead.

## Phase 3 implications

1. **Operator culture is a candidate leverage point in its own right** (Brief #2 §4): under one billing rule Yuktāhār learns from records for profit while Kadamba cooks a fixed ratio as an obligation.
