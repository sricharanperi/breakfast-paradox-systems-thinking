## Net read

Everything that could be built from *existing confirmed facts plus reasoning* is done.

required: Activity 4's "conduct interviews and observations" (bold in the framework
table, not optional) and Activity 5's "collect historical... observations" (same). No
amount of intuition closes those — they're waiting on today's/tomorrow's interviews.
