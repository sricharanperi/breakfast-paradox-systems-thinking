# Task 7 — Supporting Asset: Validation Gaps

Prioritized P0 (critical — a central finding depends on this) / P1 (important, strengthens confidence
materially) / P2 (useful, not necessary for the current diagnosis to stand).

## P0 — Critical

### P0-1: Does the CFS office's known turnout gap (35-40% breakfast vs. 70-90% other categories) ever reach registration/billing policy discussion?

- **CLAIM:** RC3 (fragmented decision rights) is why the gap persists despite being known and quantified.
- **CURRENT EVIDENCE:** The gap is known (E4); no evidence of it reaching policy-redesign level; but absence
  of evidence in this project's interviews is not the same as confirmed absence.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED:** A direct account of whether/when registration or billing policy has been discussed
  against this data internally.
- **BEST SOURCE:** CFS Chair (follow-up), or Mess Committee minutes if accessible.
- **EXACT NON-LEADING QUESTION:** "Has the turnout-versus-registration gap you described ever come up in a
  Mess Committee or CDS discussion about how registration or billing works?"
- **WHAT WOULD CONFIRM:** A direct account of such a discussion (even if it went nowhere).
- **WHAT WOULD DISPROVE:** A direct account that no such discussion has occurred, which would actually
  *strengthen* RC3 rather than weaken it, or an account that it *has* occurred and led to a considered
  decision to keep the current model — which would replace RC3's "no owner" framing with "a deliberate
  trade-off," a materially different diagnosis.

### P0-2: What is Skip Meal's actual usage rate, and does it reach kitchen forecasting distinctly from T-4 aggregate registration?

- **CLAIM:** Loop B1 is "designed but broken" — mechanism exists, closure unconfirmed.
- **CURRENT EVIDENCE:** Mechanism's existence only (from Task 6 mapping); no usage or forecasting-impact
  data in S1/S2/S5.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED:** Skip Meal declaration counts per week; confirmation of whether kitchen procurement
  uses this data separately from the T-4 registration lock.
- **BEST SOURCE:** Kitchen/vendor operations staff, or CFS Chair.
- **EXACT NON-LEADING QUESTION:** "When a student uses Skip Meal, does that change what gets ordered for
  that day, or is the order already fixed by that point?"
- **WHAT WOULD CONFIRM:** Kitchen describing a concrete adjustment process tied to Skip Meal data.
- **WHAT WOULD DISPROVE:** Kitchen confirming orders are fixed at T-4 regardless of subsequent Skip Meal
  activity — this would mean B1 is open, not merely under-evidenced.

## P1 — Important

### P1-1: Does the 30% turnout figure (Daily eater-2's estimate) match the CFS Chair's 35-40% category figure, or are they different measurements?

- **CLAIM:** P1's magnitude.
- **CURRENT EVIDENCE:** Two independent numbers in the same range from very different sources (a student's
  estimate vs. an institutional category figure) — suggestive but not the same measurement.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED:** Clarify whether "breakfast" in the CFS Chair's figure is Kadamba-only or all four
  messes, and over what time window.
- **BEST SOURCE:** CFS Chair (follow-up on methodology).
- **EXACT NON-LEADING QUESTION:** "When you say breakfast turnout is 35-40%, is that specific to Kadamba, or
  averaged across all messes, and over what period was it measured?"
- **WHAT WOULD CONFIRM:** Methodology consistent with a system-wide, sustained measurement.
- **WHAT WOULD DISPROVE:** If the figure turns out to be a single-week or Kadamba-only snapshot, its use as
  a system-wide steady-state fact in this report would need to be walked back to PARTIAL confidence.

### P1-2: Is the 5-cancellations-per-month cap accurate, and is it uniform across all four messes?

- **CLAIM:** ST2.
- **CURRENT EVIDENCE:** Single-sourced (S1 self-report).
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED:** Independent confirmation from CFS Chair or official mess policy documentation.
- **BEST SOURCE:** CFS Chair, or the registration portal's own stated policy (once the portal-URL
  inconsistency, E11, is resolved and there's a single authoritative page to check).
- **EXACT NON-LEADING QUESTION:** "What is the actual cancellation policy — is there a monthly cap, and is
  it the same across Kadamba, Bakul, Palash, and Yuktahar?"
- **WHAT WOULD CONFIRM:** Matching figure and scope from a second, institutional source.
- **WHAT WOULD DISPROVE:** A different cap number or a policy that varies by mess — either would require
  revising ST2/P2 and re-checking whether findings drawn from it (like UC2) still hold at the corrected
  scope.

### P1-3: Does the menu-transparency selective-attendance effect (R3/T3) actually show up in any attendance data?

- **CLAIM:** R3, T3, UC3, F7.
- **CURRENT EVIDENCE:** Self-identified concern only (E8), no observational data.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED:** Day-of-week or item-level attendance/turnout figures cross-referenced against the
  posted rotation schedule.
- **BEST SOURCE:** Kitchen/CFS records, if such granular records exist at all (unconfirmed).
- **EXACT NON-LEADING QUESTION:** "Do you track attendance by specific day or menu item, and if so, does it
  vary noticeably based on what's on the posted menu?"
- **WHAT WOULD CONFIRM:** A measurable attendance swing correlated with posted menu content.
- **WHAT WOULD DISPROVE:** Flat attendance regardless of posted menu — this would downgrade R3/T3 from
  "candidate" to "considered and set aside for lack of support."
