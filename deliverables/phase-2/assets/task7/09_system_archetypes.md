# Task 7 — Supporting Asset: System Archetype Fit-Testing

Per spec: test candidates, reject weak fits explicitly, do not force a fit. Two archetypes are retained
below; the rest were tested and rejected or left as unresolved due to insufficient evidence.

## Retained: Shifting the Burden — Registration/Resale (R1)

- **SYMPTOMATIC SOLUTION:** Informal resale via Mess Cell WhatsApp (ST9) — lets a ghost-registered slot find
  a user, relieving the immediate waste of that one meal.
- **FUNDAMENTAL SOLUTION (not currently pursued, per evidence):** Redesign billing/registration (RC1) so
  that registering only when intending to attend is the low-friction default.
- **SIDE EFFECT OF THE SYMPTOMATIC SOLUTION:** Resale works well enough, individually, that it removes
  pressure to fix RC1 — the visible pain of ghost registration (an unused, paid-for meal) is muted by resale
  before it accumulates into a forcing function for policy change.
- **EVIDENCE:** E7, ST9, RC1's continued unaddressed status per `08_validation_gaps.md` P0-1.
- **CONFIDENCE:** 🟡 PLAUSIBLE — the archetype's shape fits well; the specific claim that resale *causes*
  policy inertia (rather than policy inertia simply existing for unrelated reasons, e.g. RC3) is not
  independently confirmed. Both explanations may be simultaneously true and mutually reinforcing.
- **WHY RETAINED:** Every element of the classic archetype (symptomatic fix present, fundamental fix
  identifiable and named, a plausible mechanism connecting the symptomatic fix's success to reduced pressure
  for the fundamental one) has direct evidentiary support, which is the bar the spec sets.

## Tested and Set Aside (Insufficient Evidence): Fixes That Fail — December 2024 Cancellation-Cap Tightening

- **CANDIDATE:** The existing (Neha's) Task 7 draft (S7) describes a workload-driven tightening of the
  cancellation cap in December 2024, and argues this plausibly increased, rather than decreased, uncancelled
  no-shows.
- **WHY NOT PROMOTED HERE:** This entire candidate rests on a single secondary account (S7), which is itself
  another team member's independent Task 7 draft rather than a primary source in this evidence set (no
  interview, braindump, or CFS Chair statement in the material read for this analysis independently confirms
  that a December 2024 tightening event occurred, or describes its effects).
- **VERDICT:** Not rejected outright — the general shape (a workload-relief rule tightening and inadvertently
  worsening the exact problem it targeted) is structurally plausible given ST2's known cap mechanism — but it
  is held at ❓ UNKNOWN-REQUIRES VALIDATION and not written up as a confirmed archetype finding in the main
  file, per the evidence-discipline rule against upgrading a single secondary source into an established
  finding.
- **WHAT WOULD PROMOTE IT:** Independent confirmation from CFS Chair or Mess Committee that such a tightening
  occurred, plus before/after no-show data.

## Tested and Rejected

- **Limits to Growth:** No evidence of a growth-then-plateau dynamic in registration or attendance in this
  evidence set — the pattern described (steady 35-40% turnout) is a stable-state gap, not a growth curve
  hitting a constraint. Rejected.
- **Tragedy of the Commons:** Considered for the shared kitchen capacity / resource pool, but the evidence
  shows capacity constraints are not the presenting problem (turnout is *below* registration, not overshoot
  of a shared resource) — the actual dynamic runs the opposite direction from commons overuse. Rejected.
- **Success to the Successful:** No evidence of a competitive allocation dynamic between actors that would
  fit this archetype's shape (one party's early advantage compounding at another's expense). Rejected.
- **Escalation:** No evidence of two competing actors ratcheting responses against each other. Rejected.
- **Accidental Adversaries:** Considered for CFS Chair (transparency) vs. kitchen (predictability), but the
  evidence (E8) shows the *same* office holding both roles and self-identifying the tension, rather than two
  separate actors unknowingly working against each other. Doesn't fit the archetype's defining structure.
  Rejected.
- **Growth and Underinvestment:** No evidence of a capacity-investment decision being deferred in response to
  early strain signals. Rejected — insufficient data either way, not a confirmed non-fit, but not enough
  signal to write up.
