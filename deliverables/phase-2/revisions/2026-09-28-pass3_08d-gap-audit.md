## Headline verdict

The opportunity ("give this system a way to notice its own cost"), the four intervention points, their order and both gating questions **hold unchanged**. Three things need upgrading. First, the shortage constraint exists only implicitly: "Do not trade waste for run-outs" (L96) protects the late window at Kadamba, and "run-out incidents" appear in the success measure (L98), but "reduce waste without raising the chance of a shortage" is not stated as a constraint, and nothing grounds it in the operator's stated rule or in students' need for food to be there. It is added as an explicit constraint, replacing the lead of L96 and keeping its content. Second, the off-site kitchen is still a fixed ratio "70 percent, now 80 percent" with a 30-minute top-up (C3, C4, C8), and "all three operators" describe a closing rush (C7). Third, opportunity areas the Bakul evidence supports are missing: food quality as eaten after hot holding, demand by item, and menu pairing. All three are added as secondary opportunity areas derived from evidence, ranked below the four intervention points, with no solution named.

- **Activity 9, design principles:** the explicit shortage constraint belongs in **P4** (act on the first batch the day before, the morning only through top-up) and **P6** (success as cooked against eaten, run-outs and food quality): P6's measure set should read "waste falls while run-outs do not rise", and P6's quality measure should include texture and holding time, not only temperature and taste. **P5** is unchanged (no new absorber).
- **Activity 9, LP5:** at Bakul, LP5 strengthens a weekly calibration that already exists (the same placement as at Yuktāhār), with the floor as an explicit constraint and item-level cooked and eaten as its input; H7 stays a hypothesis to test, not a finding.
- **Activity 9, LP6 (crest-aware top-up):** must be per hall; at Bakul the late hedge is a held reserve because a phoned top-up across 8 km cannot arrive late in the window (candidate); LP6's staged late batch has a transport-delay variant, and the dispatch decision at the Hafizpet kitchen is the only candidate new intervention point.
- **Activity 9, LP8 (operator voice in the menu):** strengthened by the pairing evidence, but must pair operator input with consumption records and resident validation; it must never read as operator authority over students' menu choices.
- **Activity 9, LP10:** held; C5 recorded. I4 (Bakul likely neutral or favourable on LP2 if paid per served plate) enters the stakeholder impact check as candidate only.
- **Activity 10, criteria:** O3 (crest service) should become per hall and carry a shortage indicator that any option must not worsen; O6 (food quality) adds texture and holding time; O2 (cooked against eaten) is read only together with O3.
