## 0. Headline verdict

The deliverable's core move (the system is stable, the gap is absorbed before it becomes a signal, the missing piece is feedback that reaches authority) **survives and is strengthened** by the new evidence. But four of its load-bearing specifics are now wrong, and it has no account at all of the layer where physical waste is actually produced:

1. **Contradicted (P0):** "waste is currently never measured by volume, only handled by category" (§3.3). Yuktahar's Wastage Book records per item raw issued, cooked, left over, % waste, run-out time, re-cook, plus student and kitchen wastage in kg; Kadamba says waste is recorded. The precondition "measure first" is false: the missing link is **routing**, not measurement.
2. **Contradicted (P0):** "composting ... take[s] the surplus" / "waste is genuinely handled responsibly" (§2, §4). Kadamba: dustbin to garbage contractor, **no composting**.
3. **Contradicted (P0):** the mental model "procurement certainty is permanently worth more than demand accuracy" (§1) presumes the four-day lock is a procurement lock. The four-day lock is on **registration**; perishables are ordered T-1, vegetables 2-3x/week, groceries fortnightly.
4. **Contradicted/overstated (P0):** "It has the worst turnout ... at thirty five to forty five percent" (§3.1). Records: Kadamba 37.1% (April), Yuktahar ~28% mean (18-40%, Sept). The 45% upper bound is not supported by any record; Yuktahar sits below the whole stated range.
5. **Missing whole element (P1, top):** the **conversion point**, registration to cooked first batch via a private, uncalibrated ratio per operator (Yuktahar "about 50%" belief vs 28% actual; Kadamba ~70%; off-site 70-80%), which is irreversible once cooked (3-4 h life, top-up only upward). The three intervention points (billing trigger, ownership, attention) all sit on the governance layer; none sits on the kitchen layer the revised Phase 1 now puts inside the boundary.

## 5. Implications for Phase 3 (Activity 9: leverage points, rules/roles/incentives/information flows/processes, design principles, intervention points)

**Leverage points (by depth)**
- *Information flows (highest-yield):* route measured turnout (scan records, operator registers) into (a) each operator's first-batch ratio and (b) the owners of registration and billing rules. The data exists; the route does not.
- *Rules:* what a registration is (standing booking closed four days ahead vs a nearer-term decision aligned to T-1 perishables); what triggers a bill.
- *Roles / goals:* a named owner for the booked-vs-cooked-vs-eaten outcome; operators given a voice on menu items they see wasted.
- *Mental models:* "registration approximates demand" at the institution; private, uncorrected turnout beliefs at operators (50 vs 28).
- *Parameters (low leverage, but concrete):* first-batch ratio, cancellation cap, service-window end. Treat as outputs of the feedback, not levers.

**Candidate intervention points on the system map**
Registration close (T-4) → portal count read by operator → first-batch cooked quantity (point of no return) → counter / top-up loop → closing crest → surplus exits (reuse, staff meals, bin) → waste record → (missing) route to menu, registration and billing owners. Plus the missing events/exams feed into the operator planning meeting.

**Design principles the revision surfaces**
- Act before the first batch is cooked; the cooked batch is irreversible and lasts 3-4 hours.
- Route existing measurements rather than add new ones.
- Keep data close to the people who use it (Yuktahar dropped Zoho: "I want to remain close to the data"); augment operator registers, do not replace them.
- Calibrate per hall and per weekday (Sunday differs; off-site top-up lag differs).
- Never trade waste for run-outs at close; protect late-window service.
- Do not aim the fix at students or at operators; both act rationally in the current structure.
- Measure success by cooked vs eaten and by run-outs, not by turnout.
- Keep the problem breakfast-specific; lunch shows the same platform can work.
- Preserve the locally beneficial absorbers (reuse, staff meals) and the existing top-up loops; the bin is not one to protect.

**Evidence limits Phase 3 must carry**
Records for Kadamba (breakfast, April) and Yuktahar (Sept) only; Kadamba and off-site ratios and all kg/₹ figures single-sourced from noisy audio; one visit per mess, with self-presentation bias.
