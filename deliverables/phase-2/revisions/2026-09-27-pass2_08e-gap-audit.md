## 4. Phase 3 implications

1. The 70% ratio is now observer-verified, so first-batch calibration (LP shortlist item 4) can be argued from it, but per line: the veg gap (about 2.2x) is far larger than non-veg (about 1.4x); a veg-first pilot is the natural target.
2. The Phase 3 input brief's "20-30 staff" and "16/23 kg" references are superseded: the absorber to protect is ~30 portions for 30-50 outside diners plus about half of Kadamba's staff (D-45/D-49, P5). Any first-batch cut must budget for them explicitly.
3. The 5-10% vs 70%/37% tension decides whether the waste target is the bin or the held-and-reheated food. The field log (G-series gaps) should record first-batch quantity, top-ups, leftovers held, reheated and binned, and extra-diner counts on the same mornings.
4. Two forecasting philosophies side by side (Kadamba fixed ratio, Yuktāhār book learning + first-half-hour projection) make practice sharing between operators (shortlist item 7) concrete; blame-free measurement is a precondition (P2 principle).
5. Opposite operator stances under one billing rule strengthen D-54's held-back levers: Kadamba's "paid for registrations" hint suggests plates may be counted at booking; confirm before any billing-at-consumption lever.
6. Automatic, mandatory registration with a 4-day, 5-per-month exit confirms the booking rule (shortlist item 2) as the upstream lever; a T-1 confirm/skip changes a default, not a preference.
7. Oversight (facility tasting, weekly QC, 72 h samples, TDS) covers safety and taste but not quantity: an existing inspection route that could carry cooked-vs-eaten figures (information-flow lever) without new staff.
8. Yuktāhār dropped an ERP for cost and staff digital readiness: any intervention that needs digital entry by kitchen staff should build on paper books (P2 principle) and office-side digitisation.
9. The Skip Meal conflict decides whether a strengthened Skip Meal (or T-1 skip) would reach the Kadamba kitchen at all.
10. Back-door late entry that cannot be refused means any arrival-timing intervention must include the post-9:30 tail.
