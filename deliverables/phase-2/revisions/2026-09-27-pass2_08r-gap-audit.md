## Headline verdict

The stable-not-broken thesis (D-45) and the three root causes (D-50) still hold, but the statement now under-describes and in places contradicts Brief #2 in four load-bearing ways:

1. **Registration is described as a "standing weekly block"** chosen by students (lines 10, 16, 28, 88, 90, 120, 124). Brief #2 §3 corrects this: registration is **automatic and mandatory**, students are auto-registered even off campus and over holidays, and the only exit is **5 cancellations per meal type per month, each at least 4 days ahead**, with **no way to nullify the charge**. The student is not choosing a standing booking; the system assigns one. Phase 1 pass 2 already changed "standing booking" to "a default rather than a choice". P0.
2. **The extra diners are wrong** (line 49: "20 to 30 cooking and security staff eat what is left after students"; line 102: "30 to 40 extra portions go to walk-in and paying diners"). Brief #2 §3: **about 30 extra portions cooked for 30 to 50 outside people (e.g. night security) plus about 50% of Kadamba's internal staff eating from the same food**. P0.
3. **The operator is treated as one actor who measures the gap and has nowhere to send it** (lines 10, 14, 56). Brief #2 §4 shows the same billing basis produces **opposite operator stances**: Kadamba (Prism) cooks to registrations as a paid obligation with a fixed 70% ratio and no learning record beyond gram standards; Yuktāhār (ABC Hospitality) learns from a full book system and trims because waste is cost against an explicit profit goal. The "stranded information" is therefore not universal; how much is measured at all depends on operator culture. P0 in the headline, since Brief #2 directly contradicts "the catering operator measures the gap every day" for Kadamba.
4. **The Kadamba numbers and roles still carry Brief #1 status**: 70% is still tagged plain single-sourced, "the Prism team", "orders meat, eggs, milk and paneer one day ahead", "cooks by feel", no command chain, no CDS-head conflict flag, no 5-10% waste tension. P1 each, collected below.

The student voice ("We don't have any opportunity to nullify it") and the facility-team oversight (safety and taste, not quantity) are absent and are the two most useful additions for Activity 8's "Synthesize stakeholder perspectives".
